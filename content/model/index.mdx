title: Formal models
description: The Alloy model of the authority rules and the Context Mapper model of the bounded contexts.
---

The two models state intended behavior. The [system document](../(system)/system.mdx) is the product authority, and the [architecture document](../(architecture)/architecture.mdx) holds the technical boundaries.

| Model                            | States                                                                    | Check                  |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| [authority.als](authority.als)   | The role and principal algebra of authority, with its checks and mutants | `just model check`    |
| [contexts.cml](contexts.cml)     | The bounded contexts, their aggregates, and the context map               | `just model validate` |

`just guides write` renders the bounded-context part of each module guide from `contexts.cml`, and `just layout` names every context folder after one of its contexts.
